1. Introduction

Voice Changer (“Voice Changer” or the “App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect when you use the App, how we use it, and the choices you have.

By downloading or using Voice Changer, you agree to the practices described in this policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

3. Information we collect

3.1 Information stored on your device

When you use Voice Changer, the App may store locally on your iPhone:

  • Recordings and exports you create (voice clips, trimmed media, favorites)
  • App preferences — appearance, language, and feature settings
  • Subscription status needed to unlock premium features

We do not require you to create an account. We do not ask for your name, email, or phone number to use core App features.

3.2 Device permissions

Voice Changer may request access to:

  • Microphone — to record your voice for effects and related features
  • Speech recognition — when you use speech-to-text features
  • Photos / media library — when you import or save audio and video

You can revoke permissions at any time in iPhone Settings → Voice Changer.

3.3 Purchases & subscriptions

If you subscribe to Voice Changer Pro, payment is processed by Apple. We receive subscription status and related receipt information needed to unlock premium features. We do not receive or store your full payment card number.

3.4 Optional cloud AI features

Some features (such as certain text-to-speech or music generation options) may send the text or prompts you enter to third-party AI providers you or the developer configure. Do not submit sensitive personal information in those prompts. On-device Apple voices and Apple Translation can be used without sending content to those cloud providers when selected.

3.5 Analytics & advertising

We may use third-party services that collect limited technical or usage information, such as:

  • Google AdMob — if you are not subscribed, the App may show ads; AdMob may collect device and ad interaction data according to Google’s policies
  • Device type, operating system version, and App version
  • Crash or performance data if you opt in to sharing diagnostics with developers via Apple

3.6 App Tracking Transparency

On supported iOS versions, we may ask for permission to track your device across other companies’ apps and websites for advertising measurement. If you deny permission, we respect your choice and do not access the advertising identifier (IDFA) for that purpose.

4. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Provide voice effects, recording, AI speech, reverse audio, trim, and related features
  • Validate and manage Voice Changer Pro subscriptions
  • Display ads to non-subscribers and measure ad performance when permitted
  • Improve reliability, security, and user experience
  • Comply with legal obligations and respond to support requests

We do not sell your personal information.

5. Storage & retention

Recordings, preferences, and favorites remain on your device until you delete them or uninstall the App. Subscription records are retained as long as needed to provide the service and meet legal or accounting requirements.

If you contact support, we retain correspondence for a reasonable period to resolve your request.

6. Sharing & third parties

We may share limited information with:

  • Apple Inc. — App Store purchases and system services
  • Google (AdMob) — advertising as described above
  • Optional AI providers — only the content you submit to those features
  • Legal authorities — when required by law or to protect rights, safety, and security

7. App tracking & ads

You can control tracking in Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking on your iPhone. Subscribers may see fewer or no ads depending on App configuration. Denying tracking does not block free features that do not require a subscription, subject to feature limits shown in the App.

8. Your rights & choices

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent (for example, microphone or tracking permissions) at any time via device settings
  • Lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (EEA/UK users)

Because most App data is stored on your device, you can remove it by deleting the App or clearing App data. For subscription management, use Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone.

California residents may have additional rights under the CCPA/CPRA. We do not sell personal information as defined by California law.

9. Children

Voice Changer is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us information, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

10. International users

If you use the App outside your home country, information may be processed on your device and by Apple or service providers in accordance with their terms. By using the App, you understand that those providers may process data in countries where they operate.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the revised version on this page and update the effective date. Material changes may also be communicated in the App where appropriate.

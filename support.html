Frequently asked questions

How do I change my voice?

Open Voice Changer, tap record, speak, then pick an effect. Adjust intensity if available, preview, and save or share.

Why can’t the app access my microphone?

Go to Settings → Voice Changer and enable Microphone. Then return to the app and try recording again.

How do AI voices work?

Open AI Voices / Text to Speech, enter text, choose a voice and language, then generate. On-device Apple voices work without a cloud key; cloud providers need configuration by the developer.

How do I cancel Voice Changer Pro?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone: Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions → Voice Changer → Cancel Subscription. Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge.

How do I restore my purchase?

On the subscription screen in the App or in Settings, tap Restore. Make sure you are signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

What does the 3-day free trial include?

Eligible new subscribers receive Voice Changer Pro access for three days. If you do not cancel before the trial ends, Apple automatically bills the weekly subscription shown on the paywall.

How do I request a refund?

Refunds are processed by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com and sign in with your Apple ID.

Still need help?

Include your iOS version, App version, and a short description of the issue when you email us.
